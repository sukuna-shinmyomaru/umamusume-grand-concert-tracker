How to Use This Tracker

Buying Songs: Tap any song to mark it as bought. Tap again to unbuy.

Recommended Songs: Songs are sorted by priority. The recommended songs to buy are highlighted with a rainbow border.

Target Savings: Shows the total tokens needed for the highlighted songs. When there are no highlighted songs, it shows TARGET (NEXT PHASE) with savings for the next phase.

Phase Navigation: Use arrows to move between phases. The main use case is moving forward as you progress. Moving backward won't undo your bought songs - it just lets you view previous phases.

Reset Button: Clears all your progress and starts fresh.

Auto Assignments: Make Debut! is auto-assigned at Junior phase. Girls' Legend U auto-assigns when you reach 18 total songs.
