How to Use This Tracker

Buying Songs: Tap any song to mark it as bought. Tap again to unbuy.

Recommended Songs: Songs are sorted by priority. The recommended songs to buy are highlighted with a rainbow border.

Target Savings: TARGET shows the total tokens needed for the highlighted songs. TARGET (NEXT PHASE) always shows savings for the next phase's top songs.

Phase Navigation: Use arrows to move between phases. The main use case is moving forward as you progress. Moving backward won't undo your bought songs - it just lets you view previous phases.

Reset Button: Clears all your progress and starts fresh.

Auto Assignments: Make Debut! is auto-assigned at Junior phase. Girls' Legend U auto-assigns when you reach 18 total songs.

Priority Settings: Tap the gear icon to customize song priority by dragging songs up or down. Your custom order is saved automatically.

Technique Tracking: The Techniques counter shows your accumulated techniques toward the next song purchase. Tap + to increment. It resets to 0 when you buy a song or move to the next phase. The NEXT indicator shows the requirement for the next song. The required amount changes with each song bought in the phase. If you have enough techniques when moving forward, you'll carry over and get a free song purchase in the new phase. When viewing a previous phase, the technique row is hidden to avoid confusion.

Priority Settings

Customize song priority by dragging. Higher songs are recommended first.
